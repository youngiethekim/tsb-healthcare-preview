The short answer

What is medical scheduling software?

Medical scheduling software manages how patients get booked into care: appointment types, provider and room availability, online booking, reminders and the data to see how the day ran. It schedules patients, not staff shifts.

Online medical appointment scheduling

Patients book, reschedule or cancel on their own, from a link or QR code on any phone or computer, with no account and no app.

Capacity rules per site and hour

Each site sets capacity by hour, with requisition upload, group and family bookings and rebooking links.

One search across every location

Patients see open times across nearby sites in one search: earliest available, nearest, or the site that offers the service.

Reminders that refill the schedule

Email and SMS reminders with one-tap cancel or rebook, so a cancelled slot is released and offered to a waiting patient.

How it works

How does medical appointment scheduling software work?

Medical appointment scheduling software works in four steps: the patient searches open times, books in a few taps, gets reminders, and checks in on arrival.

  1. Search. One search covers every location, by earliest available, nearest, or the site that offers the service.
  2. Book. Choose a service, see open times across every nearby site, pick the earliest or the closest, and enter a name and phone number. That is four taps, with confirmation by text.
  3. Remind. Email and SMS reminders go out automatically, and one tap cancels or rebooks, so a freed slot returns to the schedule.
  4. Arrive. Patients check in by QR code or booking code at a kiosk, and walk-ins register at the same kiosk and get a ticket code.

Behind the booking, the platform holds only a name, a way to reach the patient and a time slot. No clinical content passes through it, which keeps privacy assessments light.

Who uses medical scheduling software?

Clinics and medical offices use clinic scheduling software to run the day from one calendar. Hospitals and health systems use hospital scheduling software for multi-site outpatient scheduling. Diagnostic labs use lab scheduling software for blood draws, drop-offs and kits, and imaging departments use radiology scheduling software for MRI, CT and X-ray. Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is medical scheduling software?
Medical scheduling software is the system clinics, labs and health systems use to book, change and cancel patient appointments across providers, rooms and sites. It covers appointment types, availability rules, online booking and reminders, and it schedules patients rather than staff shifts.
What is medical appointment scheduling software?
Medical appointment scheduling software is the patient-facing booking side of medical scheduling: patients find open times, book, reschedule or cancel online, and receive reminders. Slot IQ Book adds one search across every location and four-tap booking with no account.
Is medical scheduling software the same as patient scheduling software?
In practice, yes. Medical scheduling software, medical appointment scheduling software and patient scheduling software all name the system that runs the appointment book: appointment types and durations, the right provider and room for each visit, online booking, and reminders with waitlist backfill.
Does medical scheduling software include billing?
Some vendors sell combined scheduling-and-billing suites. TSB Healthcare does not do billing: there is no claims module, coding or payment processing. Slot IQ handles scheduling, booking, check-in and patient flow, and works alongside the EMR and practice systems where billing already lives.
Does it work with our EMR or lab system?
Slot IQ is integration-neutral: it works alongside any EMR, LIS or hospital information system through standard interfaces, and it is the scheduling and arrival layer rather than a new patient portal.
How long does it take to set up?
Adding a site to an existing customer takes about a day, and bringing a new organization live takes about a week of TSB Healthcare's time and a few hours of the customer's. Slot IQ is configured, not built.
