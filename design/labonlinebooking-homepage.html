I lost my booking code

We can send it again to the mobile number or email on the booking.

Can I book a LifeLabs location here?

No. This site books participating public and hospital outpatient labs. LifeLabs Patient Service Centres are booked on LifeLabs' own site, appointments.lifelabs.com. LifeLabs is not affiliated with LabOnlineBooking.ca.

I'm booking for a family member

Choose “Someone else” when you book. Use their requisition and the details on their BC Services Card, and book with their knowledge.

I need a wheelchair, an interpreter or extra time

Use the “Wheelchair access” filter in Find a lab. When you book, add a note for the lab, such as needing an interpreter or more time.

Can't book online?

Call the lab you plan to visit. Each lab's phone number is listed in Find a lab.

Do I need a requisition?

Yes. The lab needs the requisition from the clinician who ordered your test. Bring it to your visit.

How do I get my results?

This site handles bookings only. Results go to the clinician who ordered the test, and many also appear in Health Gateway.
