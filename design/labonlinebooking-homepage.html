    How it works

    From requisition to lab visit

    1. Get your requisition

      Your doctor or nurse practitioner gives you a lab requisition. It lists the tests you need.

    2. Choose test and lab

      Tell us the test and we show only labs that can do it, with the next open time at each.

    3. Pick a time

      Take the earliest opening or choose a day that suits you. Booking is free.

    4. Get your confirmation

      We text or email your booking code and a reminder before the visit.

    Prepare for your test

    What to do before you go

    Follow your clinician's instructions first. These are the usual steps.

    About 10 min

    Blood test

    • Drink water unless told not to.
    • Wear a top with sleeves that roll up.
    • Take your usual medicines unless told otherwise.
    About 10 min

    Fasting blood test

    Fasting: 10 to 12 hours
    • Nothing to eat or drink except water.
    • Book a morning time.
    • Bring a snack for after.
    About 15 min

    ECG

    • Wear a top that opens at the front.
    • Skip body lotion on your chest that day.
    2 to 3 hours

    Glucose tolerance test

    Fasting required
    • Book a time before 10 AM.
    • Stay at the lab for the whole test.
    • Bring something to read.

    Always bring: Your requisitionYour BC Services CardYour booking code

    Help

    Common questions

    Still stuck? Contact the lab you're booking with.

    Can I book a LifeLabs location here?

    No. This site books participating public and hospital outpatient labs. LifeLabs Patient Service Centres are booked on LifeLabs' own site, appointments.lifelabs.com. LifeLabs is not affiliated with LabOnlineBooking.ca.

    Do I need a requisition?

    Yes. The lab needs the requisition from the clinician who ordered your test. Bring it to your appointment, on paper or as your clinician sent it.

    Can I walk in without booking?

    Plan on booking. Some labs keep a few walk-in spaces, but waits can be long and booked patients go first.

    I lost my booking code. What do I do?

    Check your text messages and email (including spam) for the confirmation. If you can't find it, choose “Lost your code?” and we'll send it to the mobile number or email on the booking.

    How do I get my results?

    This site handles bookings only. Your results go to the clinician who ordered the test, and many appear in Health Gateway. Ask your clinician about your results.

    Is my information private?

    We collect only what the lab needs to identify you and match your requisition. Your information is stored in Canada and used for your booking.
