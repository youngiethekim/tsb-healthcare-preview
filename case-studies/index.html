The challenge & the solution

One booking experience across a fragmented lab network

Patients faced phone queues and unpredictable walk-in waits across dozens of collection sites, while staff scheduled by hand and no-shows quietly ate into capacity. TSB brought it all onto one platform.

  • 24/7 online booking across every collection site, with find-the-nearest-appointment search.
  • Self check-in & virtual queue that cleared the front desk and put walk-ins and booked patients in one queue.
  • Smart reminders by text and email, with cancelled slots released back for other patients.
  • Real-time analytics that surfaced recoverable capacity across the network.

Frequently asked questions

What is LabOnlineBooking?
LabOnlineBooking is TSB HealthCare's online lab appointment platform, deployed across British Columbia's public lab network — unifying online booking, self check-in and queue management across 69 sites, with 3.98M appointments booked since 2018.
What results has TSB HealthCare delivered?
TSB HealthCare has supported 3.98M appointments for 814,000+ patients across 69 public booking sites since 2018, at roughly 16,000 appointments a week. Leaders get live analytics on arrival, wait and completion so they can identify bottlenecks and improve patient flow.
