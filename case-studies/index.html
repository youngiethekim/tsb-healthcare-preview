The challenge & the solution

One booking experience across a fragmented lab network

Patients faced phone queues and unpredictable walk-in waits across dozens of collection sites, while staff scheduled by hand and no-shows quietly ate into capacity. TSB brought it all onto one platform.

  • 24/7 online booking across every collection site, with find-the-nearest-appointment search.
  • Self check-in & virtual queue that cleared the front desk and cut check-in time by up to 70%.
  • Smart reminders that drove the no-show rate down to 2.87% — the lowest measured in the system.
  • Real-time analytics that surfaced recoverable capacity across the network.

Frequently asked questions

What is LabOnlineBooking?
LabOnlineBooking is TSB Healthcare's online lab appointment platform, deployed across British Columbia's public lab network — unifying online booking, self check-in and queue management across 69 sites, with 4M+ appointments.
What results has TSB Healthcare delivered?
Across deployments, TSB has saved ~188K staff hours, recovered 112K+ appointments through fewer no-shows, cut check-in time by up to 70%, and delivered modeled $26M+ in operational cost savings.
