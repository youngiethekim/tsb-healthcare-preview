Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is online appointment booking software for healthcare?
It lets patients self-schedule their own appointments online, 24/7, across your locations and services — with your eligibility, routing and capacity rules built in.
Can patients book across multiple locations?
Yes. Patients book across every location and service from one system, and the platform routes them to the right site and slot automatically.
Does it fill cancellations automatically?
Yes. When an appointment is cancelled, the slot is automatically offered to other patients — recovering capacity that used to go empty.
Does it integrate with our LIS or EMR?
TSB is built to connect with your LIS, EMR and existing health systems.
