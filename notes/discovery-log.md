## 2026-09-04 — Medical imaging: why they can't do online booking

Spoke with medical imaging today. The reason they cannot do booking is that
**everything needs to be very specific**.

Unlike a general clinic slot, an imaging appointment is only valid once a long
list of specifics line up, so a generic "pick a time" booking flow can't safely
produce a bookable appointment.

### Why this matters for us

- Confirms that "online appointment booking" as a flat, one-size-fits-all slot
  picker is a non-starter for imaging.
- The gap is not willingness — it's that the booking rules are far more
  constrained than the scheduling tools they've been offered.
- Any imaging story we tell needs to lead with rule specificity (exam type,
  modality, protocol, prep, prerequisites), not with speed or self-service.

### Open questions to take back to them

- Which specifics block booking most often — exam/protocol selection, equipment
  and room matching, prep and safety screening, or order/authorization checks?
- Are the rules written down anywhere, or do they live with the schedulers?
- Which subset of exams (if any) is specific enough to be rule-encoded today?

Follow-up: get the concrete list of "specifics" before proposing anything.
