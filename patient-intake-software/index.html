What patients complete

What digital patient intake forms collect

TSB replaces clipboard paperwork with digital forms patients complete on their own device. Your patient intake forms are rebuilt from the intake workflows you already run — what you collect does not change, only how and when you collect it.

Patient information and history

Demographics, contact details and medical history arrive as structured patient information rather than handwriting. Returning patients confirm what is already on file instead of rewriting it.

Allergies and medications

Current medications, allergies and previous reactions are captured before arrival, so clinical staff open the visit with a legible list instead of reconstructing one at the desk.

Coverage and insurance information

Health plan identifiers, insurance information and any documents your site requires are collected up front and checked for completeness, so a missing field surfaces before the patient is in the room.

Consent and compliance

Consents, privacy notices and site-specific declarations are versioned and timestamped. Each submission is recorded against the form version the patient actually signed — what a healthcare compliance review asks for.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is patient intake software?
Patient intake software collects the information a visit needs — demographics, insurance details, consents and screening questions — digitally, before or on arrival, instead of on paper. TSB captures intake on mobile or at a self check-in kiosk and feeds it straight into the live queue and your systems.
Can patients complete intake before they arrive?
Yes. Patients can complete intake from the confirmation link on their own phone ahead of time, or finish it at a kiosk on arrival — so the front desk isn't handing out clipboards and re-keying forms.
Does it capture insurance and consent?
Yes. Intake captures insurance and demographic details, applies your eligibility rules, and collects the consents and signatures each visit type needs, with everything logged for your records.
Does patient intake integrate with our EHR?
TSB is built to connect with your EHR, EMR and LIS so intake data flows into the systems your staff already use.
Is patient intake data secure?
Yes. Nothing is stored on the device, the platform is PHI-minimal by design, and data is encrypted in transit. The platform is built to support HIPAA and PIPEDA obligations and BC health authority requirements under FIPPA, with data residency available in Canada and the United States.
