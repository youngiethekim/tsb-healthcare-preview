Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is a patient access platform?
A patient access platform connects every way a patient reaches care — finding, booking, checking in, waiting and being seen — into one coordinated system. TSB unifies self-scheduling, self check-in, queue management, communications and analytics.
How does TSB improve patient access?
Patients self-schedule 24/7, find the nearest appointment, check in without a line, and get called by SMS, while leaders get live data to open capacity — so waits and no-shows fall and more patients are seen.
Is it different from an EHR scheduling module?
Yes — TSB is the patient-facing access and flow layer that integrates with your EHR/LIS and adds modern self-service, real-time queueing and analytics EHR modules typically lack.
Can it scale across a whole health system?
Yes. TSB runs across dozens of sites and is built for multi-site health systems, with local control per site and a network-wide view for leadership.
