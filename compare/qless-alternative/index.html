Frequently asked questions

Is TSB HealthCare a good QLess alternative?
Yes. If you like QLess's virtual queuing but want booking, kiosks, an AI assistant and analytics in the same platform, TSB gives you the whole patient journey — book, remind, check in, queue, and measure — in one place.
Does TSB include a virtual waiting room like QLess?
Yes. Patients hold their place from their phone and are called by SMS and a now-serving display, with booked and walk-in patients blended into one fair queue.
What does TSB add beyond queuing?
Online self-scheduling, self check-in kiosks, a 24/7 AI booking assistant, automatic cancellation fill, and live flow analytics — plus a track record across millions of appointments.
