Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Is TSB HealthCare a good Kyruus alternative?
Yes — especially if you want the whole patient access and flow journey in one platform. TSB adds self check-in kiosks, a queue engine and a virtual waiting room, and real-time slot optimization on top of online booking and access analytics.
What does TSB do that Kyruus focuses less on?
In-clinic flow: self check-in, blending walk-ins and booked patients into one fair queue, now-serving displays, and automatically re-filling cancelled slots to recover capacity.
Does TSB cover provider matching?
TSB focuses on access, scheduling and flow rather than provider search. It integrates with your systems and is a strong fit for high-volume outpatient, diagnostics and imaging access.
