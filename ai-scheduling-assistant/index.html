Frequently asked questions

What does the AI booking assistant do?
It provides booking assistance in natural language, around the clock. Patients can ask questions, find a location, and book or reschedule an appointment through chat, while the assistant only offers valid slots by your rules.
Does the AI book real appointments?
Yes. The assistant books, reschedules and cancels real appointments through the same scheduling engine as your online booking, grounded in your eligibility and capacity rules.
How does this help my team?
By handling routine requests around the clock, the assistant frees your booking clerks to focus on more complex cases and help patients who are in front of them.
Is it available 24/7?
Yes — 24/7, with no hold music and no missed after-hours calls.
