Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What does the AI appointment scheduling assistant do?
It provides customer support and booking assistance in natural language, around the clock. Patients can ask questions, find a location, and book or reschedule an appointment through chat, while the assistant only offers valid slots by your rules.
Does the AI book real appointments?
Yes. The assistant books, reschedules and cancels real appointments through the same scheduling engine as your online booking, grounded in your eligibility and capacity rules.
Will it reduce call volume for my front desk?
Yes. By answering routine questions and handling bookings after hours, the assistant deflects a large share of front-desk calls.
Is it available 24/7?
Yes — 24/7, with no hold music and no missed after-hours calls.
