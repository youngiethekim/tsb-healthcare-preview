Our story

From one booking system to a patient access platform

TSB Communications Inc. was incorporated in Vancouver, British Columbia on February 1, 2011. What began in telecommunications technology grew into a company that builds and operates enterprise software. In healthcare, that work became LabOnlineBooking: a cloud-based scheduling service for diagnostic labs, clinics and health authorities. The healthcare division, TSB Healthcare, was founded in 2018 and the platform has run in production ever since.

The platform started where the pain was most visible: lab patients waiting in line for a blood draw. It grew with the teams who used it. Today it runs online booking, self check-in, queue management and flow analytics across 69 sites, including deployments with British Columbia health authorities such as the Provincial Health Services Authority and Fraser Health, alongside private clinics, diagnostic facilities and immunization centers.

We stayed deliberately hands-on: TSB develops and supports the platform itself, with in-region data residency for Canadian health-authority deployments confirmed per contract. The company is led by our Chief Executive Officer, Young Kim, from our head office at 801–838 West Hastings Street in downtown Vancouver.

What we believe

The values behind the platform

Accessible healthcare for all

A booking system only works if everyone can actually use it. Large type, high contrast, multiple languages, seated-height kiosks and a staffed alternative at every step. Accessibility is not a compliance checkbox for us — a patient who cannot use the tool is a patient who does not get seen.

Patients first

Every feature is judged by one test: does it get a patient seen sooner, with less friction? Shorter lines, fewer no-shows and clear communication are the product.

Evidence over hype

We publish numbers we can trace to real deployments (appointments booked, hours saved, no-show rates), and we report them the same way to customers and health authorities.

Privacy by design

Health data is sensitive by definition. The platform is PHI-minimal by design, offers in-region data residency, and has supported public-sector privacy and security reviews since 2018.

Reliability is a feature

Care doesn't pause for maintenance windows. Eight-plus years in production and 2,400+ appointments a day have taught us to treat uptime as a clinical requirement.

Local control at scale

One platform can serve a single clinic or an entire province, but each site keeps control of its own schedules, rules and workflows. Scale should never mean one-size-fits-all.

Partners, not vendors

We develop, host and support the platform ourselves, with no subcontractors, so the people who built your deployment are the same people who answer when you call.
