The short answer

What is healthcare scheduling software?

Healthcare scheduling software manages how patients get booked into care — appointment types, provider availability, online booking, reminders and recalls. The same phrase is also used for staff shift rostering, which is a different product entirely. TSB does the first job: it schedules patients.

Appointment types that match real visits

A new-patient consult, a follow-up and a procedure do not take the same time or need the same room. TSB models each visit type with its own duration, prep and resources, so the day you book is a day you can actually run.

Booking rules per provider and room

Availability is defined per provider, room and device, so patients can only land in slots that work. The right visit reaches the right clinician without a coordinator untangling it by phone.

Automated scheduling that runs the follow-up

Confirmations, reminders, rebooking links and waitlist backfill fire on their own. Automated scheduling in healthcare is mostly this: removing the hundred small manual steps between booked and seen.

One system across every site

Scheduling systems in healthcare tend to grow one clinic at a time until no two calendars agree. TSB runs scheduling for the whole network from one platform, with local rules where sites differ.

An honest distinction

Patient scheduling vs. staff scheduling: which one do you need?

Half the products ranked for “healthcare scheduling software” schedule employees, not patients. Before you book a demo with anyone — including us — be sure which problem you are buying for.

You schedule patients — TSB does this

Your problem sounds like: phone lines jammed with booking calls, no-shows eating clinic capacity, referrals waiting weeks for a slot, recalls missed, six sites with six different calendars. That is patient scheduling — TSB’s entire focus. Start with patient scheduling software, and see patient flow management for what happens after patients arrive.

You schedule staff shifts — look elsewhere

Your problem sounds like: building nurse rotas, covering call-outs, tracking certifications and time-off, letting staff swap shifts fairly. That is workforce management. It is genuinely useful software — and it is not what TSB makes. A dedicated staff rostering vendor will serve you better, and we would rather you find that out here than three demos in.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is healthcare scheduling software?
The term covers two different products, and the difference matters before you buy. One kind schedules patients into appointments — booking rules, appointment types, reminders and online self-scheduling. The other kind schedules staff onto shifts — rotas, time-off and coverage. TSB is the first kind: healthcare scheduling software (you will also see it written as health care scheduling software) that fills and manages the patient side of the calendar across hospitals, clinics, labs and imaging.
Does TSB handle staff scheduling or nurse rostering?
No, and we would rather tell you now than in a demo. TSB does not build shift rotas, track certifications, manage time-off requests or run open-shift swaps. If that is the problem you are solving, you need a workforce management product, and a dedicated rostering vendor will serve you better. If your problem is getting patients booked, reminded and seen, that is exactly what TSB does.
What should a healthcare scheduling solution include?
A complete healthcare scheduling solution covers the whole booking lifecycle: appointment types with realistic durations, availability rules per provider, room and device, online self-scheduling, automated reminders and confirmations, waitlist backfill for cancellations, and reporting that shows utilization by site. TSB adds what happens after booking too — check-in, intake and queue management — so the schedule and the actual day stay connected.
Is there a healthcare scheduling app for patients?
Patients use TSB in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer — no app store download, no account barrier before booking. That matters for healthcare, where many patients book once or twice a year and will not install a healthcare scheduling app for it. Staff work in the same web platform, so there is nothing to deploy to devices either.
How does automated scheduling reduce no-shows?
Automated scheduling closes the gaps people cannot: confirmations go out when the booking is made, reminders land before the visit, patients can cancel or rebook themselves in one tap, and freed slots are offered to the waitlist instead of sitting empty. Across TSB deployments that loop has recovered 112K+ appointments that would otherwise have been lost to no-shows.
Does healthcare scheduling software integrate with our EHR?
Yes. TSB is built to connect with your EHR, EMR and LIS so bookings, changes and completions stay in sync with the clinical systems your teams already use. Scheduling data flows both ways, which keeps the calendar, the chart and the front desk working from one source of truth.
