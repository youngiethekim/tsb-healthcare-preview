Benefits of patient flow management for outpatient care

The biggest benefits come from information your teams don’t have today. When arrival, wait, service and completion are recorded as they happen, the software turns every visit into information you can act on: where delays build up, which sites have open appointments, and how demand changes by hour. Staff get the information they need on one screen, and managers get reports built on real information rather than reconstructed estimates.

TSB HealthCare is built as a flexible solution rather than a fixed product. You can start with online booking, add check-in and queue functionality when you’re ready, and increase coverage site by site. That flexibility keeps implementation manageable and lets each organization prove results before scaling. It’s a patient flow solution that works alongside the systems you already use.

For managers

Reports built on what happened, not what was planned

Scheduling systems record the booking. Patient flow management records the visit — so the numbers in your report are the ones your teams actually lived.

  • Delays you can locate. See where in the day, and at which site, the wait starts to grow.
  • Capacity you can verify. Compare opened capacity against arrivals and completions, rather than estimating.
  • Information on one screen. Staff see the queue and the day ahead without switching between systems.
Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is patient flow management software?
Patient flow management software records each step of a visit — booking, arrival, check-in, wait, service and completion — so teams can see where delays build up and act on them. It goes further than scheduling, which stops at the booking.
Do we have to implement everything at once?
No. You can start with online booking, add check-in and queue functionality later, and increase coverage site by site. Each organization can prove results before scaling.
Does it replace our existing systems?
No. It is a patient flow solution that works alongside the lab, hospital and scheduling systems you already use.
Does TSB HealthCare hold clinical data?
No. The platform records the flow of a visit — appointments, arrivals, waits and completions. Medical history and other clinical information stay in your own clinical systems.
