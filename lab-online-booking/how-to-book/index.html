How to book a lab appointment

It takes about two minutes. You don’t need an account.

Before you start: have your laboratory requisition from your doctor or nurse practitioner, and your BC Services Card for your Personal Health Number.

  1. Find a lab near you

    Search by city or postal code. Choose a location that can do the test on your requisition, not only the one with the earliest opening.

  2. Choose a date and time

    Pick a day, then an open time. Times shown are live openings at that lab.

  3. Enter your details

    Your name, date of birth, Personal Health Number, and a phone number or email for your confirmation.

  4. Get your confirmation

    You’ll see a confirmation code on screen and receive it by text or email. A reminder follows before your visit.

  5. Go to your appointment

    Arrive at the lab a few minutes early with your requisition and BC Services Card. Check your requisition for fasting or other instructions.

Book an Appointment

Need to change or cancel?

Use Manage Your Booking with your confirmation code and last name. Cancelling releases the time for another patient.
