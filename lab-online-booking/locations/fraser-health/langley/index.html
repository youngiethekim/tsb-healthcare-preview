Not sure this lab can do your test? Check your requisition or ask the clinician who ordered it.

Before you go

  1. Bring your requisition from your doctor or nurse practitioner.
  2. Bring your BC Services Card or other photo ID with your Personal Health Number.
  3. Check for fasting or other instructions on your requisition.
  4. Arrive 5–10 minutes early. If you can’t make it, cancel or change your booking.
