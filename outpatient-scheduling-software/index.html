Frequently asked questions

What is outpatient scheduling software?
It lets patients book, reschedule and check in for outpatient appointments while giving departments a real-time view of flow and capacity. TSB combines self-scheduling, self check-in, queue management and analytics in one platform.
Which outpatient departments does it support?
Any high-volume outpatient service — diagnostic labs, imaging, specialty and referral clinics, pre-op, immunization and community programs — each with its own appointment types and rules.
How does it reduce outpatient wait times and no-shows?
Self-scheduling and self check-in, one fair queue for walk-ins and booked patients, automated reminders and instant cancellation-fill cut waits and recover no-shows.
Does it integrate with our EHR or LIS?
Yes — TSB connects with your EHR, LIS and existing systems.
