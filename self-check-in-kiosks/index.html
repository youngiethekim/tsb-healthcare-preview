Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is a patient self check-in kiosk?
A patient self check-in kiosk is a self-service station where patients confirm their details and check in for their appointment on arrival, without waiting for a front-desk agent. TSB kiosks then place the patient straight into the live queue.
How do self check-in kiosks reduce wait times?
Kiosks remove the single biggest bottleneck at the door — the front-desk line. Patients check in the moment they arrive, staff are freed for the patients who genuinely need help, and everyone flows into one managed queue.
Are TSB kiosks accessible and multilingual?
Yes. The check-in flow is designed to be accessible and available in multiple languages, so every patient can self-serve with confidence.
Can patients check in without an appointment?
Yes. Kiosks handle both booked patients and walk-ins, blending them into one fair queue.
Do kiosks store protected health information?
The flow layer is designed to operate without holding protected health information on the kiosk, and data is encrypted to support HIPAA, PIPEDA and U.S. & Canadian data residency.
