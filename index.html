What changes

What it feels like when patient flow works

The numbers above are the outcome. This is what they look like on a Tuesday morning, for the people who actually run the day.

Nothing goes to waste

A patient who cancels three days out is a slot you can still fill. One who simply doesn’t arrive is a slot you paid for twice. Reminders turn the second into the first, and the waitlist fills it before anyone notices it was empty.

Your team gets its job back

Before, a receptionist processes arrivals one at a time while everybody behind them waits, and stops every few minutes to answer “how much longer?” After, they handle the people who genuinely need a person. Same team, more patients, less firefighting.

You can finally answer the question

How full are we this afternoon? Which site is running hot right now? Is Thursday always like this? For most organizations that takes a spreadsheet and a week, which means the decision gets made on instinct. It shouldn’t have to.

Patients stop watching the door

The worst part of waiting isn’t the time, it’s not knowing whether you’re next or ninetieth. Give someone their place in line and the freedom to wait where they choose, and the same wait becomes a completely different experience.

Our mandate

Accessible healthcare for all

That is the slogan, and it is also the test every screen has to pass. We set out to build products a 70-year-old or a 9-year-old could use without being shown how — and we hold the people deploying them to the same standard.

How it works

One connected patient journey, end to end

Nine steps from the clinician’s requisition to the supervisor’s dashboard. Every one of them is the same system, so nothing is re-keyed and nothing is estimated after the fact.

Before the visit

Patient & referring clinician
  1. 01RequisitionThe clinician hands over a QR code, or books on the patient’s behalf there and then.
  2. 02BookThe patient finds a nearby collection site and a time that suits them, at any hour.
  3. 03ConfirmedConfirmation arrives with a reference number, plus any preparation the test requires.

At the site

Patient, kiosk & queue
  1. 04Check inThe patient enters their code at the kiosk. No queue at the counter, no clipboard.
  2. 05Wait anywhereThey’re given a recommended return time and can wait where they like — an estimate the queue keeps updating, not a promise.
  3. 06CalledTheir number appears on the now-serving screen, and by SMS if they stepped out.

After the visit

Technician & operations
  1. 07SeenThe technician takes the patient. The visit is matched to the right room and service.
  2. 08LoggedArrived and completed are marked as they happen, so timings are recorded rather than reconstructed.
  3. 09AnalyzedSupervisors see wait times, throughput and where the day actually loses time — by site, by hour.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is patient flow?
Patient flow is how patients move through a visit — booking, arrival, check-in, waiting, being seen and leaving. Good patient flow means shorter waits, less crowding and better use of staff and rooms. TSB Healthcare unifies booking, self check-in, queue management and analytics to keep that flow moving.
What does TSB Healthcare do?
TSB Healthcare is a patient flow and access platform for hospitals, labs and clinics. It unifies online booking, self check-in kiosks, patient intake, smart queue management, AI scheduling and real-time analytics — cutting wait times, recovering capacity and lowering operating costs.
What is Slot IQ™?
Slot IQ™ is TSB Healthcare's scheduling, arrival and intelligence platform for health systems: online booking, an AI assistant, self check-in kiosks, queue management and analytics. It runs everything between the decision to book and the moment a patient is seated — behind the front doors health systems already have.
Does TSB Healthcare work across multiple locations or a whole health authority?
Yes. TSB scales from a single clinic to a regional or state/province-wide health authority, running every site from one platform with local control and a shared view.
How does TSB Healthcare reduce wait times and no-shows?
Patients book and check in themselves, walk-ins and booked patients blend into one fair queue, automated reminders cut no-shows, and leaders get live analytics to fix bottlenecks. Deployments have cut check-in time by up to 70% and driven no-show rates as low as 2.87%.
Is TSB Healthcare secure and compliant?
TSB is PHI-minimal by design: the booking and flow layer holds a patient's name, email and phone only, with no health numbers, requisitions or results. Data is encrypted in transit, the platform has supported health-authority Privacy Impact Assessment and security reviews since 2018, and an independent penetration test was completed in 2024. There is no certification for HIPAA, PIPEDA or FIPPA; the platform is designed to support them. See our security & compliance page for what we do and do not claim.
