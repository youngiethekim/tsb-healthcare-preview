The problem we solve

Scheduling shows what was planned. Patient flow shows what happened.

Outpatient demand keeps rising, and much of the capacity healthcare organizations open is already spoken for. Most scheduling systems stop at the booking. They can’t tell you when a patient arrived, how long they waited, or when service was complete, so it’s hard to identify bottlenecks or improve patient flow. TSB HealthCare captures each step on a single platform, so teams can reduce waiting times, improve resource utilization and make decisions on real-time data instead of estimates.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Can patients search across multiple labs or health authorities?
Yes. Patients search once and see available appointments across every participating location, instead of checking each lab or organization separately. Each organization keeps control of its own sites, services and hours.
What is patient flow?
Patient flow is how patients move through a visit — booking, arrival, check-in, waiting, being seen and leaving. Good patient flow means shorter waits, less crowding and better use of staff and rooms. TSB HealthCare unifies booking, self check-in, queue management and analytics to keep that flow moving.
What does TSB HealthCare do?
TSB HealthCare is a patient flow and access platform for hospitals, labs and clinics. It unifies online booking, self check-in kiosks, patient intake, smart queue management, AI scheduling and real-time analytics — cutting wait times, recovering capacity and lowering operating costs.
What is a digital front door in healthcare?
A digital front door is the set of online tools patients use to find, book and check in for care — online booking, self-scheduling, reminders and self check-in. TSB HealthCare is that front door, connected to a queue and analytics engine behind it.
Does TSB HealthCare work across multiple locations or a whole health authority?
Yes. TSB scales from a single clinic to a regional or state/province-wide health authority, running every site from one platform with local control and a shared view.
How does TSB HealthCare reduce wait times and no-shows?
Patients book and check in themselves, walk-ins and booked patients blend into one fair queue, automated reminders cut no-shows, and leaders get live analytics to identify bottlenecks and improve patient flow across every site.
Is TSB HealthCare secure and compliant?
Yes. TSB is designed to run without holding protected health information, with encryption and support for HIPAA, PIPEDA, FIPPA and U.S. & Canadian data residency.
