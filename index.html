The slot queue engine

Maximize bookings. Free up space.

The engine continuously fills open and cancelled slots with walk-ins and recovered bookings — lifting utilization, turning no-shows back into seen patients, and clearing the waiting room.

  • More bookings from the same hours — every open slot put to work.
  • Less wasted space — a virtual waiting room keeps the room clear.
  • Fewer no-shows — cancellations are re-filled automatically, protecting revenue.
