Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is a patient queue management system?
A patient queue management system organizes everyone waiting to be seen into a single, fair, real-time queue — blending booked appointments, walk-ins and reserved slots — and calls patients in order via displays and SMS.
What is a virtual waiting room?
A virtual waiting room lets patients hold their place in line from their phone and wait wherever they're comfortable instead of a crowded waiting room. They get an SMS when it's nearly their turn.
How do you handle walk-ins and appointments together?
TSB blends booked patients, walk-ins and reserved slots into one intelligent queue with fair prioritization, so there are no separate lines and no guesswork.
How are patients notified when it's their turn?
Patients are called by a now-serving display in the facility and by SMS to their phone, so the waiting room stays calm.
