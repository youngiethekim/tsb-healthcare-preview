Frequently asked questions

How is this ROI estimated?
It applies TSB's measured results across 68+ live sites to your inputs — reducing no-shows toward ~2%, cutting average wait time ~40%, and recovering front-desk time via self check-in — then multiplies recovered appointments by your revenue per visit and staff hours by a loaded hourly cost.
Are these numbers a guarantee?
No — it's a directional estimate to size the opportunity. Request the full report and we'll build a tailored, per-site projection with you.
What counts as a 'recovered appointment'?
An appointment otherwise lost to a no-show or unfilled cancellation that is kept or refilled through reminders, easy rescheduling and automatic cancellation fill.
Can I get an estimate for my exact sites?
Yes. Request the full breakdown and we'll email an itemized, per-site ROI report and benchmark, and a specialist can walk your team through it.
