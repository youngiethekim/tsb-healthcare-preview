The short answer

What is a lab booking system?

A lab booking system — or laboratory booking system — lets patients find, book and check in for laboratory services online — blood draws, lab tests, sample drop-offs and kit pick-ups — across every collection location, instead of phoning a central line or queueing at a counter.

A laboratory booking system shows real availability, by location

Patients see genuine open capacity at each collection location, not a contact form. Availability comes from the rules the laboratory already runs — service type, appointment length, staffing and opening hours — so a booked slot is one the site can actually keep.

It handles the requisition up front

Where your lab requires one, patients can record their requisition details while booking, so the front desk confirms a lab test order rather than transcribing it on arrival. Fewer patients turn up for the wrong service, or without the paperwork the draw depends on.

It covers every lab service, not just draws

Routine and fasting blood tests, specimen drop-offs, kit collection and timed collections each carry their own duration and preparation rules, so the schedule reflects how long the work actually takes instead of assuming one slot length fits everything.

It hands off to your LIS

TSB schedules the visit and manages the queue; your laboratory information system stays the system of record for specimens and results. Patients receive results through the channel your laboratory already uses — TSB does not sit in that path.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Does TSB support online lab appointment booking?
Patients book lab appointments online across every collection site, 24/7, with your eligibility and capacity rules built in.
Can it handle walk-ins and drop-offs?
TSB blends booked draws, walk-ins, sample drop-offs and kit pick-ups into one fair queue with self check-in so the front desk stays clear.
Is it built for high-volume outpatient labs?
TSB has processed millions of appointments across dozens of high-volume lab sites, and scales from a single collection site to an entire network.
Can I book blood tests online?
With TSB, patients book blood tests and other lab appointments online across every collection site, 24/7 — choosing the nearest available slot instead of calling or walking in.
What is lab scheduling software?
Lab scheduling software runs the appointment book for a diagnostic laboratory: it defines each service and how long it takes, publishes availability by collection location, lets patients book themselves online, and keeps the day moving with reminders, self check-in and one queue for booked and walk-in patients. It schedules people and capacity — the laboratory information system still owns specimens and results.
Do patients need a requisition to book a lab test?
That depends on your laboratory's rules, and TSB follows them rather than replacing them. Where a requisition is required, patients can enter its details during booking so the collection site knows which lab tests are ordered before the patient arrives. Where a service is open-access, patients simply choose it and book.
Can patients choose which collection location and time?
Patients see availability across every collection location in the network and pick the site and time that suits them — including a nearby location with an earlier opening. When a site's capacity changes, the availability patients see changes with it.
How does it handle collection sites with both walk-ins and appointments?
Booked patients, walk-ins and sample drop-offs join one ordered queue instead of competing lines, and self check-in registers arrivals without a counter conversation. Staff see the real queue for the site and patients see where they stand, so no station sits idle while another backs up.
