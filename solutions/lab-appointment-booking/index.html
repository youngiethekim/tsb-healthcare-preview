Lab scheduling software

One search across every participating laboratory

Most lab booking tools make patients check one location at a time. With TSB HealthCare, patients search once and see open appointments for lab tests across every participating laboratory, then pick the location that’s most convenient. It’s easy for any user, on any device, and every user sees the same simple steps.

For lab management teams, the same data shows demand by location, day and hour, so resource utilization and staffing can follow real patient volume. Appointment management stays simple: each site sets its own hours, services and resource limits. That means better access for patients and better use of every resource the laboratory already has.

Since 2018, Lab Online Booking™ has supported public outpatient lab booking at 69 sites in British Columbia.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Can patients search across more than one laboratory?
Yes. A single search returns open appointments across every participating laboratory, so patients do not have to check each site in turn. Each organization keeps control of its own sites, services and hours.
Does each site keep control of its own schedule?
Yes. Every site sets its own operating hours, services and resource limits. The shared search only ever offers appointments that site has opened.
What information does booking collect?
Name, contact details and the appointment information needed for the visit. Medical history and other clinical information stay in your own clinical systems.
Is this available in Canada?
Yes. TSB HealthCare is built and supported in British Columbia, and Lab Online Booking has supported public outpatient lab booking there since 2018.
