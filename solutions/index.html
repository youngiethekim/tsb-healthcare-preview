Frequently asked questions

What patient flow solutions does TSB Healthcare offer?
TSB is one platform with self check-in kiosks, online appointment booking, patient queue management and a virtual waiting room, an AI scheduling assistant, and patient flow analytics — plus SMS communications and LIS/EMR integration.
Can I start with one solution and add more later?
Yes. Most teams start with their biggest bottleneck — often self check-in or online booking — and expand across the platform as they grow.
Which solution is right for my setting?
Labs and imaging usually start with online booking and self check-in; hospitals with queue management and analytics; public health with high-volume booking and check-in. Book a demo and we'll map it to your operation.
